Course Plan

Topic
Photography — composition, light and timing
Source
21 Photography Tips for Intermediate Photographers — Spencer Cox, Photography Life
Why
It teaches the decisions behind a good photo — composition, light and timing — rather than gear, so every tip can be practised with the camera I already have.
Each week
Study one tip, shoot outside class to test it, then edit, review and write my reflection in the Tuesday period.
Course changes
None so far.
