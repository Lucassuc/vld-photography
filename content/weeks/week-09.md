Since the weather has been clear lately, I took an older photo from a sunny day and edited it to look rainy. I wanted to see how weather changes a photo’s mood. The original felt energetic, but I had trouble explaining why. In the rainy version, the mood feels clearer and fits the scene better to me. Still, editing the weather is different from photographing it myself. Next time, I want to take a photo in real rain and see if I can capture that mood in camera.
