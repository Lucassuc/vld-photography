This time, I didn't raise my camera immediately to take the photograph. I put it down and thought about the scene I wanted to capture, and I noticed two lines, one at the top and one at the bottom of the frame. Then I pictured the shot in my mind. I imagined a car passing through, and I planned to capture it when the car reached the middle. So I raised my camera and waited. When a car came, I took the shot. This was a completely different feeling from raising my camera immediately. This time I had a purpose and knew what I wanted before I took the photo, which made the photo intentional.
