I usually shoot at a comfortable middle distance, but this week I moved much closer to the subject. The one in the middle now looks clearly bigger than the two next to it, even though they're about the same size, and the depth of field became shallower. At 28mm and f/4, the background and side pomelos blur while the face stays sharp, so the subject stands out more. The hardest part was getting the focus exactly on the eyes, since a small movement threw it off, so I steadied myself and refocused each time.
