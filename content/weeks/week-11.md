I saw these two elders walking side by side last weekend and wanted to capture a warm, loving moment between them. They sometimes walked at different speeds, so I followed from a distance and waited for them to slow down and walk together again. I caught them from behind, with the busy street around them. The moment feels close, but next time I’ll try to frame them more clearly against the street.
