Last week, I practiced waiting for the right moment before taking a photo. This week, I used that patience to look for shapes around our school and noticed the window could frame the large archway outside. I imagined the scene first, then tried to line the window edges up with the photo’s borders. My hands kept shaking, so I slowed down and held my breath. The frame connects the window and arch, but I could line up the edges more carefully. Next time, I’ll steady my hands and check the borders before I shoot.
