This time, I thought about how the light matched the mood of the street. Even though the question says not to assume golden light is always best, I think it fits this scene. I noticed how the warm light fell on the columns and how the long shadows stretched across the sidewalk. It made the street feel calm and familiar, which was the feeling I wanted to capture. At first, I focused on the patterns of light and shadow because they caught my attention. Then I thought about what they added to the photo. The light was not just making the street look beautiful; it was helping me show its mood. Next time, I want to photograph the same street in different light and compare how it feels.
