This is where I usually wait for the school bus, so I chose it for this week's scouting practice. On this mostly clear morning, sunlight came from the left, while the buildings left patches of light and shadow across the crossing. I wanted to capture the girl entering the sunlight, but I felt uncomfortable photographing a stranger up close, so I kept a wider view. The crossing's lines lead toward her, but she is still in shadow, so I didn't fully capture the lighting I wanted. I found a useful angle, but I need more precise timing. Next time, I'll return around the same time on a clear morning, check where the light reaches, and frame the shot before someone enters.
