This is where I usually wait for the school bus, so I know the morning sunlight comes from the left. For this week's scouting practice, I noticed how the buildings block some light, leaving bright patches across the crossing. The sky was mostly clear, making the light and shadows stand out. I wanted to capture the girl walking into the sunlight, but I didn't know her and felt uncomfortable photographing her up close, so I kept a wider view. I used 28mm, f/8, and 1/320s. Next time, I'll return around the same time on a clear morning and choose a position where the crossing's lines lead toward someone in the light.
