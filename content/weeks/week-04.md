For portraits, I always thought the subject was the key: if it stood out enough, the background didn't matter. This week's tip changed that. I checked the edges of the frame before shooting and cleared out anything cluttering the background, and the photo came out cleaner right away. I hadn't realized how much a messy background pulls attention away, even when the subject itself is strong. Clearing the edges didn't just tidy up the shot. It made the subject stand out more than it would have on its own.
