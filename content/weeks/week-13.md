Last week, I looked for shapes that connected the window and the arch. This week, I wanted my photo to feel lonely. The courtyard looked quiet and empty, and the fog made the buildings and colors seem softer. The wet tiles reflected the white and soft light, while the railings framed the open space. I hoped someone would walk through the courtyard and give me a clear subject, but no one came. At first, I felt disappointed. Then I saw that the empty space made the photo feel even more lonely. The sky takes up a lot of the picture, though, so next time I’ll frame lower and wait longer for someone to walk through. I want to see how having a person in the scene changes its mood.
