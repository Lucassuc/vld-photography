# Source: Spencer Cox, "21 Photography Tips for Intermediate Photographers", Photography Life
# https://photographylife.com/photography-tips-for-intermediate-photographers
01 | - | Planning and outlining the course | Choosing the source, planning one tip for each week, and setting up this website.
02 | 21 | Memorize Your Camera | Learning every button and setting so exposure and focus become automatic and never slow down a shot.
03 | 4 | Simplify | Removing anything from the frame that weakens the photo's main message.
04 | 8 | Watch the Edges | Checking the borders of the frame for distractions before taking the photo.
05 | 10 | Put It Into Perspective | Moving closer to or farther from the subject to change how large objects look compared with each other.
06 | 20 | Think About Your Scene Abstractly | Seeing a scene as shapes, lines and colours, and balancing them in the composition.
07 | 5 | Find Good Light – For Your Subject | Choosing the light that matches the subject's mood instead of assuming golden hour is always best.
08 | 12 | Scout | Visiting a location in advance to plan when and where to return for the best conditions.
09 | 19 | Don't Avoid Bad Weather | Shooting in rain, fog or heavy cloud to use the strong mood that bad weather creates.
10 | 15 | Capture Your Subject Doing Something | Photographing a subject in action so the image tells a story.
11 | 17 | Wait for Patterns | Staying after a missed moment, because many scenes and movements repeat.
12 | 11 | Look For Interconnectedness and Visual Puns | Finding similar shapes or colours between elements that tie the photo together.
13 | 2 | Focus On Emotion | Making every decision about light, composition and colour on purpose to strengthen the feeling of the photo.
14 | 7 | Refine Your Photos in the Field | Taking several versions of a scene and improving the composition with each shot before leaving.
15 | 1 | Don't Follow the Rules | Breaking common composition rules when doing so makes the photo stronger.
16 | 18 | Be Selective | Reviewing the term's photos and showing only the strongest ones.
